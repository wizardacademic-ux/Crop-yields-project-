Fund Committee Meeting Notes -- Drought Resilience Program Budget Review
(Informal notes, not circulated for approval)

Attendees: Programme lead, two committee members, one field coordinator.

Programme lead: We've confirmed the budget cut means we can only carry three
of the four crops into the drought-resilience program this cycle. Need a
recommendation on which one to drop, backed by the historical data, before
the board meeting.

Committee member A: My instinct says keep Cotton no matter what -- it's by
far our largest crop by area and production in Gujarat, so cutting support
there feels like the wrong optics even if the numbers say otherwise
elsewhere.

Committee member B: I'd push back on dropping Maize. Market prices for maize
have been trending up the last couple of seasons and a few of our
partner cooperatives specifically asked about maize-focused support at the
last outreach meeting. Feels like bad timing to pull it.

Field coordinator: For what it's worth, I remember Groundnut had a rough
year in 2015 because of a pest outbreak, not weather -- don't want that
getting confused with a rainfall story if it comes up in the data.

Programme lead: Understood, but the mandate here is specifically about
which crop's yield history shows it would benefit most from a
drought-resilience investment -- irrigation support and drought-tolerant
seed R&D -- versus one that's already comparatively insulated from rainfall
swings. Cooperative interest and current commodity prices are a separate
conversation for the marketing team; let's keep the resilience-program
recommendation itself tied to the yield and rainfall history.

Committee member A: Fair. Let's see what the analysis says.
